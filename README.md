Turn your own photos into a GTA-style sunset poster cover — multi-panel
layouts, neon sunset grades, bold condensed lettering — and export it as a
PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080. The app is fully offline: your
photos are read on your machine and never uploaded.

What it does:

- **5 layouts** — full-bleed hero, hero + 3 panels, 2×2 grid, 9-panel mosaic, and a tilted snapshot strip.
- **6 sunset grades** — Vice Sunset, Neon Pink, Palm Dusk, Heat Haze, Noir Heat, Golden Hour — applied per panel as a gradient-map grade, plus film grain, vignette and halftone dots.
- **Any photo, per panel** — add one or several photos, click a panel to select it, click a thumbnail to fill it, drag to reposition, zoom to crop.
- **Bold caption lettering** — editable title and subtitle in vendored Anton and Bebas Neue (SIL OFL), with a neon outline, rising-sun disc and gold rule.
- **One-click export** — PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080, or all three at once.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Add a photo (or try a sample), pick a
layout and a grade, type your caption, and export.

Everything is original work: no Rockstar logo, artwork, character or font.
Fonts are Anton and Bebas Neue under the SIL Open Font License (see
`app/fonts/OFL.txt` and `app/fonts/OFL-BebasNeue.txt`).
